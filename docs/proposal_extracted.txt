4. Problem Statement

What challenges do users/customers face?

Elderly individuals aged 60 and older may experience serious safety incidents while living alone, including falls, prolonged immobility, abnormal temperature, and other potentially dangerous events.These incidents may not be detected immediately when no caregiver is present. Current monitoring solutions have limitations: smartwatches are difficult for the elderly to use and have short battery lives (1–2 days), leading users to forget to charge or wear them; cameras compromise privacy by storing raw video in the cloud, suffer from high alert latency due to internet dependency, and have high false-alarm rates (failing to distinguish between sleeping and an actual fall). Notably, there is currently no system that integrates multiple sensors for cross-verification of incidents, nor is there a mechanism to automatically transmit video evidence and rapidly connect to family members or emergency services (115) when an accident occurs. 

What is the impact of this issue?

Delays in detecting incidents cause elderly people to miss the “golden time” for emergency medical treatment, resulting in a high risk of death or permanent disabilities (traumatic brain injuries, hemiplegia, bone necrosis). For families, caregivers are constantly anxious and worried when working far away. For healthcare facilities, hospitals and emergency medical teams (115) face difficulties due to receiving information late and lacking historical vital-sign data (heart rate, SpO2, fall progression) before the patient arrives at the hospital, reducing the effectiveness of initial diagnosis and treatment. In addition, standalone monitoring devices frequently generate false alarms, causing unnecessary overload for the healthcare system and families.

Why did the team choose to solve this problem?

The team aims to apply Edge AI technology combined with Multimodal Sensor Fusion, including visual cameras, thermal matrix sensors, audio analysis, image analysis, and wearable devices. This solution helps accurately detect incidents, eliminate false alarms, protect the privacy of images at home, and immediately send alerts along with vital-sign data to family members, authorized persons (family doctors), and the hospital emergency system (115 hotline), thereby optimizing medical intervention time and improving the quality of safety protection for elderly people.

6. Objectives and Scope

6.1 Objectives

6.1.1 Building a Multimodal Health Monitoring Ecosystem 

S (Specific): Design and fully develop an integrated health monitoring system combining Edge AI (running on an Orange Pi 5 single-board computer), a cloud backend, and a mobile application (React Native). The system integrates four sensor channels—standard camera with audio, an AMG8833 thermal sensor, and a BLE vital-sign tracking wristband—to automatically detect falls, strokes, high fever, and respiratory distress. 

M (Measurable): 

Edge AI (YOLO) pose estimation inference speed reaches at least 30–35 frames per second. 

Response time from incident occurrence to emergency alert activation is under 3.0 seconds. o Latency for MQTT alert packet transmission from the Edge Hub to the server and mobile application is under 500 ms. 

The system will be evaluated using at least 50 controlled test scenarios covering falls, immobility, abnormal temperature, abnormal heart rate, low SpO2, and multimodal combinations.

A (Achievable): With a team of software engineers proficient in Python, FastAPI, React Native, and IoT/AI processing, and a 3.5-month implementation timeline, completing the hardware-software prototype is entirely feasible. 

R (Relevant): The product directly addresses safety concerns for elderly individuals living alone, alleviates psychological burdens on families, and optimizes hospital emergency response times. 

T (Time-bound): Complete the entire development, system integration, and field testing phases prior to the final evaluation deadline.

6.1.2  In-scope

Function

Hardware-accelerated or optimized Edge AI model for 17-keypoint skeletal pose estimation and immobility detection( Orange Pi 5 using the integrated Rockchip RK3588S NPU ). 

AI-based audio event classification for distress sounds, calls for help, screams, and significant impact sounds.

Contactless thermal screening to estimate forehead surface temperature and identify abnormal temperature patterns. 

 Data collection (heart rate, SpO2, and impact acceleration) from a BLE wearable device. 

 Multimodal sensor integration engine designed to minimize false alarms. 

 High-priority emergency push alert system that overrides silent mode on the caregiver's device. 

Automatic extraction of Short incident video clips extracted from a configurable circular media buffer around the detected event capturing the incident scene upon a "Red Alert" trigger.

 Dashboard for monitoring time-series vital sign data and one-touch SOS (115) emergency calling. 

 Live monitoring and playback: Supports live camera streaming (Live View) and a timeline slider (Web/Mobile) to review the rolling 1–2 day video history recorded at the Hub. 

User

Elderly User: Beneficiary of automated safety monitoring, receiving localized voice reminders from the Hub when forgetting to wear the wristband.

Caregiver / Family Member: Receives red alert SOS alarm sirens, views incident video clips, monitors real-time vital signs, and places 115 emergency calls.

Family Physician / Administrator: Sets vital sign alert thresholds, reviews medical history logs, and configures hardware devices.

6.2 Scope not included

Deep Medical Hardware Intervention: The system does not replace specialized intensive care unit (ICU) therapeutic equipment, such as mechanical ventilators, defibrillators, or 12-lead electrocardiogram (ECG) machines.

Direct Integration into Emergency 115 Telecommunications Infrastructure: Emergency calls and location data dispatched to 115 emergency services are triggered through automated emergency speed-dial protocols via the caregiver’s mobile application, without directly interfacing with national emergency dispatch core systems.

Legally Binding Automated Medical Diagnostics: Vital sign and body temperature alerts function strictly as early-warning remote monitoring tools and do not provide diagnostic medical conclusions in place of certified physician evaluation.

Large-scale Commercialization: The project focuses on research, system integration, and testing of a functional Prototype/Beta version, excluding recurring subscription billing models, mass commercial production, or e-commerce payment gateways.

Performance & Latency

- The edge skeleton pose estimation speed must achieve at least 30 FPS.

- The on-device fall detection latency must be less than 3.0 seconds.

Security & Privacy

- All AI vision inference processing must be executed locally on the Edge Hub, ensuring raw video streams are never continuously transmitted to the Internet.

- Media uploads are strictly limited to authenticated incident images/short clips.

- Media transmissions and WebRTC/HLS/MQTT connections must be encrypted via TLS/SSL and authenticated using JWT.

NFR03

Usability

- The mobile app (React Native) and Web Dashboard user interface (UI) need to be intuitive, clean, and newbie-friendly.

- The search and filtering features in the system should be quick and easy to use, featuring interactive timeline sliders for historical video review.

NFR04

Reliability & False Alarm Filtering

- The home Edge Hub must operate continuously 24/7, with automatic service restoration (Systemd Daemon) upon power recovery without requiring manual operation by elderly users.

- The system must maintain a True Positive Rate (TPR) ≥ 95% and keep the False Positive Rate (FPR) < 2% using a multimodal Sensor Fusion cross-verification mechanism.

NFR05

Scalability & Storage Tiering

The backend system (FastAPI + EMQX Broker + Redis) is containerized via Docker and needs to be scalable to handle thousands of home Hubs without performance issues.

- Tiered storage strategy: Local Hub storage (SD Card/USB) automatically overwrites in a circular (FIFO) loop after 24–48 hours; critical incident videos on Cloud Object Storage are automatically purged after 30–90 days.

13. Potential Risks and Mitigation Strategies

Categories

Risk Name

Potential Risks

Mitigation Strategies

Technology

Sensor Failure / Noise

AMG8833 thermal sensors, obscured cameras, or disconnected/dead BLE bands cause the system to collect insufficient monitoring data.

• Implement a Heartbeat check mechanism and handle sensor errors instantly.• Set up a fallback logic: If 1 sensor is missing, the remaining channels are still active to issue a conformity alert. • Play a voice prompt at the Edge Hub when it detects that the user forgot to wear the BLE ring.

Edge AI performance on Orange Pi 5 (Edge Performance)

Simultaneous processing of camera frames, thermal matrix, and audio analysis on the Orange Pi 5 may experience overload, lag, or overheating of the NPU.

• Optimize the AI model through conversion to ONNX/RKNN format to get the most out of the Rockchip NPU hardware (RK3588S). • Reduce the input resolution sufficiently (320x320 / 640x640) to ensure a minimum inference speed of 30 FPS.

System

Network & Power Outage

A sudden loss of Internet connection or power outage interrupts the sending of FCM alerts to the relative's app and the Cloud.

• AI processing and 100% local storage of rolling data (RAM/SD Card) at the Edge Hub.• Automatically re-synchronize crash data to the Cloud as soon as the Internet connection is restored.• Configure the service to automatically start (Systemd Daemons) as soon as power is restored without manual intervention.

Security & Privacy

Video & Data Privacy Leaks

Videos of the elderly's personal activities in the home are leaked or illegally accessed via the Internet.

• Never stream raw continuous video to the Cloud; all AI Vision processes 100% at Edge Hub.• Only extracts and encrypts 5-second clip uploads to the Cloud when there is a red alert issue.• Uses TLS/SSL protocols, JWT encryption, strict access permissions (RBAC), and automatically deletes data after the specified deadline.
